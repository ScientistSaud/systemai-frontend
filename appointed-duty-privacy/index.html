Privacy Policy
Appointed Duty: Prayer Alarm

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Appointed Duty ("the app") is a prayer-alarm app that reminds you before each prayer and jama'ah. This policy explains what the app accesses, why, and what it does not do. It is written to be honest and plain. If anything here is unclear, contact us at the email below.

The short version

  • The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • The app has no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party trackers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information for marketing.
  • Your settings stay on your device. We do not run a server that stores your personal data.

What the app accesses, and why

Location (approximate, precise, and in the background).
The app uses your location to work out accurate prayer times for where you are and to help you find nearby mosques. To do this, your location or a place name you type is sent to open mapping services (OpenStreetMap Nominatim for turning a place name into coordinates, and OpenStreetMap Overpass for finding nearby mosques). Background location, where you grant it, lets prayer times stay correct as you move. Your location is used only for these features. We do not store it on any server of ours and do not use it to identify or profile you.

Internet.
The app connects to the internet to fetch prayer and jama'ah times from prayer-time providers (for example mawaqit.net) and, where relevant, individual mosque websites. These requests retrieve times; they do not send your personal information.

Notifications and alarms (post notifications, exact alarm, full-screen intent, wake lock, vibrate, foreground service, run after restart, ignore battery optimisation).
These let the app ring a reliable alarm at the right time, show it over the lock screen, and keep working after your phone restarts. They are used only to deliver your prayer alarms. No data is collected through them.

Calendar (read and write).
Only if you choose to add prayer times to your calendar, the app writes those entries to the calendar on your device. It does not read your calendar for any other purpose and does not send calendar data anywhere.

On-device storage.
Your preferences (such as your chosen mosque and alarm settings) are stored locally on your device using the app's own storage. This information stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Third-party services

To provide the features above, the app sends requests to these independent services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim and Overpass) for geocoding and finding nearby mosques: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy
  • mawaqit.net for mosque prayer and jama'ah times: mawaqit.net
  • Individual mosque websites you select, for their published times.

We share only what is needed to return a result (for example a place name or coordinates). We do not send them your identity, because the app does not hold your identity.

Data we collect and share (summary for the Play data safety section)

  • Collected by us on a server: none. The app has no backend that stores your data.
  • Location: used and sent to the mapping/prayer-time services above to provide the feature; not stored by us.
  • Shared for advertising or analytics: none.
  • Data stored on your device only: your app settings.

Children

The app is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • You can deny or revoke location, notification, and calendar permissions at any time in your device settings. Some features (accurate local times, reliable alarms, calendar entries) depend on the relevant permission.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the settings stored on your device.

Security

Because the app keeps your settings on your device and runs no personal-data server, there is no central store of your information to be breached. Requests to third-party services are made over secure (HTTPS) connections.
